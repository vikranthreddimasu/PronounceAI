## Design Context

### Users
PronounceAI is for English learners and student researchers who want immediate pronunciation feedback and local voice rendering without accounts or paid third-party services. They are likely recording short lines repeatedly and need the interface to stay calm while the models do heavy work in the background.

### Brand Personality
Clear, intelligent, restrained. The product should feel like a modern local AI lab: capable, private, fast, and quiet.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean app-first interface, not an editorial notebook. Favor readable sans-serif typography, neutral surfaces, direct labels, and one obvious next action per screen. Avoid repeated feature entry points, oversized serif headlines, dense control clusters, and decorative complexity.

### Design Principles
1. Show each feature once: Practice scores speech, Voice Lab renders cloned voice, Progress reviews history, Settings handles preferences.
2. Keep the primary workflow visible and push secondary controls behind progressive disclosure.
3. Make recording the voice profile available where it matters, but never let it block typing or listening.
4. Use typography for clarity before personality: larger body text, clear hierarchy, no tiny critical labels.
5. Preserve advanced model capabilities while making the default path feel simple.
